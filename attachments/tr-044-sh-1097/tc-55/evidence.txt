WHY SKIPPED AND NOT FAILED
There is no control to lock, unlock or leave ON, so none of the three scenarios
has a subject. The behaviour TC-55 wants is now delivered by a different
mechanism (the per-rule Membership Program GATE), whose own coverage lives in
TC-51.3 and in the fix13 gate tests. Marking these `failed` would report a
product defect where the product intentionally changed shape.

ACTION: retire TC-55, or rewrite it against the per-rule gate. The gate's
"forced ON and locked" analogue is that a gated rule HIDES Redemption, the When
toggle, and the Overall / Per-Person-Daily / Overall-Daily counters, replacing
them with a passive tile -- already covered.
