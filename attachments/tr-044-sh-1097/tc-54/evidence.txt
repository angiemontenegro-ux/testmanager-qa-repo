THE PREDICATE
RulesLimitsStep.tsx:1195-1200
  const blocksExit = (ruleId) => ruleCardBlocksExit({
      isCommitted: !addedRuleIdsRef.current.has(ruleId),
      isDirty: dirtyRuleIdsRef.current.has(ruleId),

"Uncommitted" is `!isCommitted || isDirty` -- the cards' OWN predicate, and
"the same one that decides whether a card renders its `Unsaved` marker"
(:1172-1174). findUncommittedRuleCard() then names the first offending card, in
on-screen order (Ticketing, Memberships, Shop) so the refusal points at the card
an admin scrolling down would reach first.

The guard is stricter than the TC asks for: it refuses NEXT, PREVIOUS and SAVE.
:1186-1189 records the defect that motivated it --
  "a rule from `+ Add Rule` enters the draft on the press, mounts uncommitted
   and untouched, badges itself `Unsaved` -- and SAILED THROUGH SAVE AND NEXT,
   shipping a rule nobody confirmed and then locking `+ Add Rule` through
   universal exclusivity."
:1192-1197 records that Ticketing was folded in as a behaviour change: its rows
used to be committed FOR the admin by flushPendingTicketingRows on the way out;
a ticketing card holding uncommitted edits is now refused like its siblings.
